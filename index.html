A LITTLE CONTEXT 01

Understanding can help.
So can staying close.

Reframing asks, “Is there another way to see this?” Reparenting adds, “What did I need then, and how can I offer some of that now?”

There is room for both. You do not need the perfect words or a big breakthrough. Start with what feels manageable.

FOUR WAYS TO CARE 03

A PRACTICE, NOT A TEST

Small acts add up.

Explore one pillar at a time. You can come back whenever you need.

01⌂

Steady care

Keep a small promise to yourself. Routine can be a way of saying, “I’ll be here.”

02✿

Room for play

Make time for something enjoyable, messy or curious without needing it to be useful.

03≈

Emotional steadiness

Name what is happening. Find one thing your body can rest against, even for a moment.

04♡

Self-compassion

Try speaking to yourself as you would to someone you love who is having a hard day.

A MINUTE TO ARRIVE

Let the next breath be enough.

A GENTLE ROUTINE 04

RETURN TO WHAT YOU NEED

A ritual you can return to.

There is no perfect way to do this. Let it be short, ordinary and yours.

01

Arrive

02

Listen

Ask softly: “What do I need right now?” Give yourself a moment for an answer.

03

Respond

Choose one small way to meet that need. It does not have to fix everything.
